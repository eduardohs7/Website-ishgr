14TH EDITION - BELÉM/PA - 2027

About the event.

The International Conference (CHAGS) is one of the most traditional and relevant global events in the fields of Anthropology, Archaeology, and Human Ecology.

Held under the auspices of ISHGR, the 14th edition consolidates itself as a strategic space for debate, training, and the dissemination of scientific knowledge focused on sustainability, bioeconomy, and traditional peoples, with the Amazon as the central stage for contemporary discussions.

Target audience.

CHAGS 14 is aimed at researchers, anthropologists, archaeologists, students, as well as indigenous leaders, policymakers, and professionals in the socio-environmental sector.

The conference promotes integration between the global academic community, traditional knowledge, and civil society.

INTERNATIONAL SOCIETY

About ISHGR.

The International Society for Hunter-Gatherer Research (ISHGR) is the leading global organization dedicated to the study, preservation, and dissemination of research on hunter-gatherer societies.

History and Mission

Since its founding, ISHGR has been the meeting point for anthropologists, archaeologists, ecologists, and traditional populations. The society promotes the ethical exchange of knowledge, respecting the autonomy and wisdom of indigenous peoples.

With each edition of CHAGS, the society renews its commitment to inclusive science, bringing urgent debates on land demarcation, food sovereignty, bioeconomy, and climate resilience to the center of global discussions.

CHAGS Milestones.

Throughout its history, the Conferences have gathered thousands of participants, establishing themselves as the premier forum on ancestral societies.

1966

Symposium "Man the Hunter" in Chicago (USA), the founding event.

1978

CHAGS 1 in Paris (France). First official conference of the series.

2022

CHAGS 13 in Dublin (Ireland).

2027

CHAGS 14 hosted in Belém/PA (Brazil). For the first time in the Amazon.
