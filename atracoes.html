PANEL

Prof. Dr. Maria de Fátima Souza

FEDERAL UNIVERSITY OF PARÁ (UFPA)

Social Technologies and Inclusion in Riverside Communities

With a PhD in Ecology, the researcher will bring discussions on social innovations applied to bioeconomy.

Full Professor at UFPA, she has been working for over 20 years with riverside communities in the Amazon estuary, seeking social innovation solutions that connect academic rigor with real value for local populations.
