About the Event

A global gathering on the banks of the Guamá River.

The 14th International Conference of the ISHGR (CHAGS 14) stands as the premier global forum for research on traditional populations, ancestry, and human ecology.

With a central theme focused on sustainability and social innovation, the event in the Amazon connects academia, leaders, and civil society to design collaborative structural solutions.

OFFICIAL EVENT

CHAGS 14: A 50-year legacy arrives in Belém

From July 12 to 16, 2027, the capital of Pará will host the 14th ISHGR Conference. Continuing a historic legacy, the event will gather global researchers and activists in an inclusive space to discuss the future of hunter-gatherer societies.
