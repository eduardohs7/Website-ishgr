About the Event

A global gathering on the banks of the Guamá River.

The 14th International Conference of the ISHGR (CHAGS 14) brings together an international community studying hunter-gatherer societies, human histories, and ecological relationships.

Hosted in the Amazon, the conference creates space for researchers and communities to exchange perspectives, share knowledge, and discuss the challenges and resilience of foraging peoples.

Global Research

Presentations and comparative perspectives from researchers around the world.
