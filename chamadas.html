Call for Papers.

In its 14th edition, which will be held from July 12 to 16, 2027, in Belém-PA, Brazil, the event will accept brief documents on works in progress or potential topics for roundtables, sessions, and conversation circles.

FOR AUTHORS

Submission instructions.

Submitted works must be in the format of brief explanatory documents (3 to 5 pages in PDF), containing research abstracts or proposed topics for the event's roundtables.

Submission is exclusively electronic through the Participant Area. Accepted languages are Portuguese, English, and Spanish.
